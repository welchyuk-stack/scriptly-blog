## Common questions

<div class="faq">
<details open><summary><h3>Do I need to know how to code?</h3></summary><p>No. Every template is ready to run — you just fill in a few fields like a folder, an email address or a time. On Macs with Apple Intelligence you can also describe what you want in plain English and Scriptly picks and fills in the right template. You never have to read or write AppleScript unless you want to.</p></details>
<details><summary><h3>Does Scriptly run scripts automatically?</h3></summary><p>Only if you schedule one yourself. Nothing runs until you press Run or set a schedule, and scripts that could change or delete files ask for confirmation before they run.</p></details>
<details><summary><h3>Which Macs does Scriptly support?</h3></summary><p>Scriptly requires macOS 14 or later. The optional AI features (template matching from a description, Explain and Fix) need macOS 26 on a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on; everything else — templates, running, scheduling, the menu bar and the Marketplace — works without them.</p></details>
<details><summary><h3>Does the AI need an internet connection?</h3></summary><p>No — the AI features run on-device using Apple Intelligence, so what you type isn't sent anywhere.</p></details>
<details><summary><h3>Do scheduled automations run when the app is closed?</h3></summary><p>Yes. While Scriptly is open it runs schedules on time; when it's closed, a lightweight background helper checks every 15 minutes and catches up on anything that's due. Scheduled automations also start as soon as Scriptly opens, and a job never runs twice.</p></details>
<details><summary><h3>What's the Marketplace?</h3></summary><p>A community library where you can share your own templates and download other people's, on top of the 115 built-in ones. Every shared script is scanned for risky commands before you run it, and you can report a template or block its author.</p></details>
<details><summary><h3>Scriptly vs Shortcuts or Automator?</h3></summary><p>Shortcuts and Automator are Apple's free automation tools and are great for many jobs. Scriptly is for AppleScript-based automations: it gives you 115 ready-made, fill-in-the-blanks templates, built-in scheduling, menu bar quick-run and on-device AI help to find, explain and fix scripts. Plenty of people use both.</p></details>
<details><summary><h3>Is there a free tier or a subscription?</h3></summary><p>Neither — Scriptly is a ${{ site.sections.scriptly.price }} (£{{ site.sections.scriptly.gbp_price }}) one-time purchase with everything included. No subscription, no in-app purchases, and every new template we add is included.</p></details>
