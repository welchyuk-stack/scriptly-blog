# TopBox Support

TopBox is a menu-bar-only app for Mac — look for its icon in the menu bar after launching (there's no Dock icon or main window). Press the global hotkey (default **⌘⇧T**) or click the menu bar icon to open the panel.

## What TopBox does

- **Clipboard history** — automatically captures text, images, and code-like text as you copy, with full-text search and one click to copy any past entry back to the pasteboard.
- **File Shelf** — a fixed drop zone for temporarily parking files while you work; drag files in, search by name, drag them back out to Finder or another app whenever you need them.

## Common questions

### How do I change the hotkey?

Open Settings from the menu bar panel and click the hotkey field to record a new combination.

### Can I stop TopBox from clearing my clipboard history?

Yes, two ways: pin up to 3 favorite entries (pinned items are never cleared), or set auto-clear to "Never" in Settings. You can also raise the history limit up to 500 items.

### I dragged a file into the shelf — where did it go?

Nowhere — the File Shelf doesn't move or copy the file, it just gives you a stable drop target to hold a reference to it. Drag it back out to Finder or another app, or right-click it to Open, Show in Finder, or Remove it from the shelf.

### Does TopBox collect any data?

No — no account, no analytics, no tracking, no network calls of any kind. Everything is held in memory only, on your Mac. See the [Privacy Policy](/topbox/privacy/) for details.

### What does "Reset All Data" do?

Clears your entire clipboard history and file shelf. It's confirmation-gated since it can't be undone. "Clear All Pinned Files" is different and non-destructive — it just unpins files, it doesn't remove them from the shelf.
