# PortHole Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 2026*

PortHole is a menu-bar utility for Mac. This policy covers what it does and doesn't do
with your data.

## What PortHole does not do

- **No account, no sign-in.** PortHole doesn't know who you are.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.** Nothing about your usage is collected or
  transmitted anywhere.
- **No data ever leaves your Mac**, with one exception below.

## The one exception: AirPlay on your local network

PortHole's AirPlay button uses Apple's built-in AirPlay system to find and connect to
displays on your local network, which is why macOS may ask for local-network
permission. Discovery and casting are handled entirely by macOS; PortHole doesn't see,
collect, or transmit anything about the devices found, and nothing goes beyond your own
network.

## What PortHole stores locally

Everything below stays in this Mac's local preferences storage — never transmitted,
never backed up to iCloud, never seen by us:

- **Your saved display arrangement** (if you use that feature) — which monitor goes
  where, identified by the monitor's vendor/model/serial number, not by anything
  identifying you.
- **Your preference settings**, such as auto-fix-on-connect and launch-at-login.

## Third-party access

PortHole reads information about your connected monitors — resolution, vendor, model,
serial number — to detect and fix resolution mismatches. None of this involves a third
party or leaves your Mac.

## Purchases

PortHole is a one-time paid download on the Mac App Store. Apple processes the purchase
entirely through its own systems — PortHole never sees your payment details. See
[Apple's own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for how Apple handles
that transaction.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Material changes
will be reflected in the app's release notes.
