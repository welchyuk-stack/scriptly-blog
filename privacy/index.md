# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 2026*

MacBound ({{ site.url }}) is a static content site. This page explains what data is collected from visitors.

## Analytics

This site uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/), a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies or collect personally identifiable information. It reports aggregate page-view and performance data only.

## No accounts, no tracking cookies

Reading any post, guide, or reference page on MacBound requires no login, no account, and sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

## Third-party links

Guides on this site link to third-party sites (including the Apple App Store for Scriptly and SnipClip, and GitHub). Those sites have their own privacy policies, which this policy does not cover.
